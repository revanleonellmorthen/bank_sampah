# D1. Topic & Problem Statement

## 1. App Name & Description
* **App Name:** **EcoSmart Waste Bank** (*Digital Waste Management Platform*)
* **One-Paragraph Description:** EcoSmart Waste Bank is a digital waste management platform designed to streamline community recycling operations and point-based savings. The system enables residents to register waste deposits, track categorized waste weights (plastic and non-plastic), and accumulate digital points—featuring a dynamic weekend multiplier bonus. Users can redeem their collected points for shopping vouchers, mobile credits, or partner discounts, creating a transparent, highly accessible, and engaging circular economy ecosystem.

---

## 2. The Problem
* **Who has it?** 
  1. *Urban Residents / Campus Community:* Individuals who want to recycle household waste and track their recycling savings digitally but face rigid operating schedules and cumbersome procedures.
  2. *Waste Bank Officers / Administrators:* Operators burdened by manual bookkeeping, physical ledger entry, and error-prone monthly reporting.

* **How do they deal with it today?** 
  Conventional waste banks rely heavily on paper-based logbooks and physical passbooks (*buku tabungan fisik*). Residents must bring sorted waste to physical stations during limited operating hours, wait in queues for manual weighing by officers, and have transactions jotted down by hand.

* **Why is that painful?**
  1. *Limited Accessibility:* Rigid schedules conflict with daily working hours, discouraging participation.
  2. *Human Error & Low Transparency:* Manual bookkeeping is prone to calculation errors, misplaced records, and lost passbooks, which erodes trust in savings balances.
  3. *Lack of Engaging Incentives:* Static and opaque reward systems fail to build long-term recycling habits among citizens.

---

## 3. Target Users / Actors
* **Resident:** The primary end-user who logs waste deposit requests, tracks real-time point accumulation, and redeems rewards via the platform.
* **Waste Bank Officer:** The operational staff member responsible for inspecting deposited waste at collection points, inputting weights into the system, and verifying deposit transactions.
* **System Administrator:** The high-level manager who configures master data, manages waste category point rates, updates partner reward directories, and audits system reports.

---

## 4. Scope
* **What the app WILL do:**
  1. Enable digital recording of sorted waste deposits (plastic vs. non-plastic) and exact weights in kilograms inputted by officers.
  2. Calculate dynamic rewards based on business rules:
     * *Weekdays (Mon–Fri):* 3 points/kg for plastic, 2 points/kg for non-plastic.
     * *Weekends (Sat–Sun):* Double points (6 points/kg for plastic, 4 points/kg for non-plastic).
  3. Manage reward redemptions through an integrated catalog of vouchers, mobile credits, and discounts.
* **3 things the app WILL NOT do:**
  1. *Will not provide automated hardware/IoT sensor integration* (deposit weights are inputted manually by the officer via the web/mobile interface).
  2. *Will not manage industrial recycling manufacturing processes* (tracks quantities and point valuations only, not physical factory processing).
  3. *Will not offer direct cash withdrawals or e-wallet cash-outs* (redemptions are limited to vouchers, credits, and partner products).
